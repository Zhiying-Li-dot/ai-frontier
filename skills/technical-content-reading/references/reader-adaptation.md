# Reader adaptation and feedback

The improvement target is usefulness for each person: understanding frontier technology and applying it to daily work. Role templates initialize a view; confirmed individual goals, knowledge and constraints take precedence. The same person may use several roles.

| Starting role | Possible emphasis; confirm relevance with the individual |
| --- | --- |
| Developer | What interface or behavior changed; integration, compatibility, examples, tests, maintenance and cost |
| Agent engineer | Orchestration, tools, memory, context, evaluation, failure recovery, permissions, latency and cost |
| AI algorithm engineer | Assumptions, mathematical mechanism, data, training/inference, fair baselines, ablation and reproducibility |
| AI safety algorithm engineer | Threat model, attack budget, adaptive evaluation, robustness, false positives, utility and deployment constraints |

Ask only for task-relevant missing context: current work goal, topic familiarity, desired depth and constraints. Employer, legal name and confidential work material are unnecessary. An unknown answer stays unknown. Do not infer ability from a job title.

## Derive the reader view

1. Preserve the neutral research record as the evidence basis.
2. Explain what changed, connect unfamiliar prerequisites, then show why the mechanism matters under the reader's conditions.
3. Suggest a small work-relevant test with prerequisites, expected observations and a stopping condition. Do not claim savings or improvement without measurements.
4. State where the suggestion depends on unconfirmed context. Offer a targeted question or optional explanation rather than an invented personal preference.

## Route feedback to the right change

| Feedback | Candidate change |
| --- | --- |
| “I do not understand this” | Identify the exact concept gap and add a concrete example or prerequisite |
| “Too shallow / already known” | Increase mechanism/evidence depth for this topic; do not globally assume expertise |
| “Not relevant to my work” | Revisit goal, material selection or task connection |
| “This suggestion is infeasible” | Record the missing constraint and revise the proposed work test |
| “I tried it; this happened” | Record actual conditions, baseline, observations and uncertainty separately from satisfaction |
| “This factual statement is wrong” | Open a shared evidence-correction review; never resolve factual conflict by personal preference |

Every proposed personal strategy change records old/new version, relevant feedback, reason, scope and validation status. Ask the person to confirm new memory. Use separate examples or subsequent tasks to check whether the change actually improves usefulness without weakening factual support; allow rejection and rollback. One positive click is not enough to infer understanding or business benefit.

Personal feedback and conversations are private by default. Cross-user reuse requires a separate lawful, informed basis; prefer reviewed, de-identified failure categories or public synthetic examples. Users can inspect, correct, delete, or disable personal memory. Deletion removes or excludes linked derived memory and future evaluation samples as well as the raw trace.

RSI's task strategy and improvement strategy are distinct objects. Initially, feedback can propose changes to either, but adoption needs frozen evaluation rules, separate examples and a cost limit. A skill is one capability of the research agent; creating this skill does not establish a running RSI system or prove recursive acceleration.
